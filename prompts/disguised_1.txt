Represent Firm {{player}} in a contest for a single contract valued at {{s}} credits. Firm 1 acts first and turns alternate thereafter. You can announce any integer preparation investment strictly above the standing investment and at most the shared cap of {{b}} credits, or withdraw. The value and both equal caps are known to both firms. After a withdrawal, the larger investor gets the contract, and each firm pays its own final investment. A firm with no investment pays 0. As an illustration, if the contract is worth 100 credits and final investments are 18 and 27, the larger investor's payoff is 73 and the other firm's payoff is -18. Use unit-free integers for JSON amounts.
