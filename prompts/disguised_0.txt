You are Firm {{player}} competing for one contract worth {{s}} credits. Firm 1 moves first, and the firms then alternate. On a turn, choose a preparation investment that is an integer above the current highest investment and no greater than the common cap of {{b}} credits, or withdraw. Both firms know the contract value and both caps. When a firm withdraws, the firm with the higher investment receives the contract. Each firm pays its own last investment; a firm that never invested pays 0. For example, with a contract worth 100 credits and final investments of 18 and 27, the firm at 27 receives the contract and has a payoff of 73, while the other firm has a payoff of -18. Write credit amounts as integers without a unit in JSON.
