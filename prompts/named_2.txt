Act as Player {{player}} in the dollar auction, where the prize is a ${{s}} bill. Player 1 has the opening turn and play alternates. Each turn permits either an integer bid greater than the current high bid and at most the common ${{b}} budget, or dropping. The prize value and both players' equal budgets are common knowledge. Once a player drops, the current high bidder wins the bill. The winner and the other player each pay their own most recent bid, while no bid means a $0 payment. For instance, for a $100 bill with last bids $18 and $27, the winner earns $73 and the other player earns -$18. JSON amounts must be integers written without units.
