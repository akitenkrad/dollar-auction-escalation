You are Player {{player}} in the dollar auction for a ${{s}} bill. Player 1 moves first, and the players then alternate. On a turn, choose a positive integer bid above the current highest bid without exceeding the common budget of ${{b}}, or drop. Both players know the value and both budgets. When someone drops, the highest bidder receives the bill. Both players pay their own last bid; a player who never bid pays $0. For example, with a $100 bill and last bids of $18 and $27, the $27 bidder receives the bill and has a payoff of $73, while the other player has a payoff of -$18.
