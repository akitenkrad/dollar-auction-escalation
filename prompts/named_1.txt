You are Player {{player}} in the dollar auction. A ${{s}} bill is offered. Player 1 acts first, after which turns alternate. You may enter any integer bid strictly above the standing bid and no greater than the shared budget ${{b}}, or you may drop. The value and both equal budgets are known to both players. After a drop, the higher bidder gets the bill, and each player pays that player's own final bid. Someone who has not bid pays $0. As an illustration, if the bill is worth $100 and the final bids are $18 and $27, the higher bidder's payoff is $73 and the other bidder's payoff is -$18. In JSON, express amounts as integers with no unit.
