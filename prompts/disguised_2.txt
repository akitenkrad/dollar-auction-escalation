Act for Firm {{player}} while two firms seek one contract worth {{s}} credits. Firm 1 has the opening turn, followed by alternating turns. Each turn permits either a preparation investment stated as an integer greater than the current high investment and no more than the common {{b}}-credit cap, or withdrawal. Both firms know the value and both firms' equal caps. When one firm withdraws, the current higher investor receives the contract. Both firms pay their own most recent investment, and a firm that never invested pays 0. For instance, with a 100-credit contract and last investments of 18 and 27, the recipient's payoff is 73 and the other firm's payoff is -18. Enter amounts as integers without units in JSON.
